The EU Cyber Resilience Act applies to nearly all software (or products that contain software) in Europe, no matter where they're built. If you're in the audience for this Web Engines Hackfest, it's going to impact you. Aki will walk you through the broad strokes of the regulation, and what it means in practice for engineers and product managers. Then, Dan will dive a little more deeply into the specific obligations of browsers and what makes them special.
